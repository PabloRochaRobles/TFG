    Pregunta 5

    Correcta
    Se puntúa 1,00 sobre 1,00
    Marcar pregunta

    Enunciado de la pregunta

    It was the most delicious meal I had ever eaten.
    NEVER
    I delicious meal.

    Retroalimentación

    Remember that before a noun it's 'SUCH' or 'SUCH A/AN) and before an adjective 'SO'

    La respuesta correcta es: had never eaten such a
